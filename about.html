Jhianne Kaydee Redulla

Welcome to KD’s anikssories! I’m Kaydee, the founder of this shop. I created this shop mainly because I enjoy putting anik-aniks on my things, such as my phone, lanyards, or laptops which can add a flavor on my items. Ever since, I’ve had this imagination that “What if I create my own anik-aniks?” To build the concept of this shop, I followed a lot of inspirations to make sure that every anikssory I make will complement well, such as making the colors and details feel balanced yet playful at the same time. This is my imagination turning into reality!

Whether you’re dressing up or just keeping it casual, anikssories will help you add color to your look. Putting and creating designs for anikssories is my secret to happiness, so I hope that every piece will let you shine and bring happiness wherever you go! KD’s anikssories is not just about accessories – it’s about adding a little joy to your everyday life.
